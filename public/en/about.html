Our journey

From a bedroom to an agency.

We didn't start with an office or a big team. We started with the drive to learn and to solve real problems for the businesses around us.

  1. The beginning

    It all started in my bedroom.

    With one computer, many hours of learning and a clear dream: to have my own software agency and build technology for businesses in my area.

  2. First projects

    Real businesses, real needs.

    The first websites were for nearby businesses: a local eatery that needed to take orders via WhatsApp and a barbershop that wanted to look as good online as it does in person.

  3. Our own product

    Our loyalty cards are born.

    We saw that many businesses had no way to bring customers back. So we created our digital stamp and points cards that customers save on their phones.

  4. Today

    A software agency for Guanacaste.

    We build websites, Google rankings, online stores, social media, custom software and loyalty cards. The dream keeps growing, one project at a time.
