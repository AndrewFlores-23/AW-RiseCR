Frequently asked questions

What you should know about SEO.

Do you guarantee first place on Google?+

No. No one can guarantee it, because Google decides the results. What we do is put your business in the best position to show up.

How long until I see results?+

It depends on your industry and local competition. Changes on Google aren't immediate; we'll explain what to expect after reviewing your case.

Do I need a website?+

For local SEO a good Google profile is enough, but your own website helps a lot. Our website plans already include basic or local SEO.

What is a Google profile?+

It's the listing that appears on Google and Google Maps with your hours, location, photos and reviews.
